2. DATABASE CLEANUP & PERFORMANCE OPTIMIZATION (PHASE 2)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- [ ] Database Schema Unification:
      • Create a unified Postgres VIEW `players` backed by `profiles` (linked to `auth.users(id)`).
      • Avoid double-writing across `profiles` and `players` tables.
- [ ] Automated TTL Queue Cleanup:
      • Schedule SQL function/cron (`clean_stale_matchmaking_queue`) to auto-expire
        matchmaking queue entries older than 2 minutes.
      • Auto-expire pending challenges older than 15 minutes.
- [ ] Compound Database Indexes:
      • Add composite index on `games (status, white_player_id, black_player_id)`.
      • Add composite index on `moves (game_id, move_number ASC)`.
      • Add composite index on `friend_requests (sender_id, receiver_id, status)`.
- [ ] Automated `updated_at` Postgres Triggers for games and challenges.
